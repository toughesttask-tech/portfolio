Excluder

Instagram feature concept · Mobile UX / UI

Problem: spoilers for shows and movies, and sensitive content, show up in feeds without warning. Instagram lets you follow hashtags to see more of what you like. Excluder is the opposite of that includer: a feature that lets you block hashtags, so unwanted spoilers and sensitive posts never reach your feed. A small, user-controlled filter that makes Instagram calmer and safer to scroll.
